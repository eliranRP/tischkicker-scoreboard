Local table-football scoresheet. Two teams. First to 5 or 10. Phone on the rail. Not a soccer live-score, not a video game.

How to keep score
• Choose first to 5 or first to 10.
• Tap a goal.
• Undo the last goal if the tap was wrong.
• Start a new match in one tap.

v1 stays on the iPhone. No account, no tracking, no photos, no network.

Delete the app to wipe the match. There is no cloud copy.
