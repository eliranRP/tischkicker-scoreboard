Privacy Policy — Foosball Scoreboard

Local scoresheet. We collect nothing.

EN DE FR NL

Last updated: 1 September 2026

This app is a local table-football scoresheet. It keeps the current match on your iPhone. It does not require an account.

What we collect

Nothing.

The app does not create an account, does not sign you in, and does not send your score, name, or device data to us or to anyone else. Team names you type stay on the device. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising identifier, no crash reporter, and no third-party tracker.

Permissions

The app does not use camera, photos, microphone, location, contacts, or tracking permission.

Internet

v1 does not need a network connection to keep score. If a later version adds optional features that use the network, this policy will be updated before that ships.

Children

The app is a utility, not directed at children, and it does not collect personal data from anyone.

Your choices

Delete the app to remove all local match data. There is no cloud copy to delete.

Contact

Questions: GitHub issues, or the support email on the App Store listing for this app.

Changes

We will post updates to this page if the app starts collecting data. v1 does not.
